FAQ

Frequently asked.

Can’t find the answer? Write to edin@nokto.no.

What happens after I pay?

You go straight to the download, and you get an email from us within one working day.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now.

Can my client see my data?

No. Everything is stored locally on your PC. The client only sees the report you choose to export and send.

Do I need GitHub?

GitHub provides deliveries and projects automatically. Time from your PC, VS Code and Claude Code is recorded without it too.

Is the app in English?

The app itself is in Norwegian today. Client reports can be created in English or Norwegian.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that dangerous?

No. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows does not recognise it. Choose “More info” and “Run anyway”.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The subscription is paid one year in advance and renews automatically. Email edin@nokto.no before the renewal date and we stop the renewal. You keep access for the period you have paid for. Periods already paid are not refunded.
