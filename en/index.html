Sources

From git log to invoice.

Five sources merge into one timeline. Projects are detected automatically.

windows-activity
Active or idle, and which program is in front (file name only, e.g. Code.exe). Never window titles or what you type.
vscode
Editor sessions, via the extension.
claude-code
Sessions, via hooks.
git
Repo and branch for the folder you work in. Commits are not read locally.
github
Commits, pull requests, releases and CI.
The problem

Your timesheet doesn’t lie. It just says nothing.

You shipped commits, pull requests and releases. The timesheet says “development 7.5 h”. DEVPROOF connects your time to your deliveries, so the client sees the work – and you no longer have to defend the invoice.

How it works

Four steps. No timesheet.

  1. 01 $

    Install

    Install the app on Windows and connect VS Code, Claude Code and GitHub.

  2. 02 $

    Work as usual

    Your working day is recorded from Windows activity, VS Code, Claude Code and Git. Projects are detected automatically.

  3. 03 $

    See your day

    The timeline shows your whole day. Click a time span to see what documents it. Client, hourly rate and deliveries are gathered per project.

  4. 04 $

    Send the report

    PDF, CSV or JSON, in English or Norwegian, with your logo and colour. Estimates are always labelled as estimates.

Privacy first

Your work. Your machine.

Built for developers who refuse to be monitored.

privacy.tomlnot a real file, but the rules the app follows
screen_recording = false
keylogging       = false
never_stored     = ["code", "prompts", "passwords"]
storage          = "local SQLite"
sent             = "licence key, at start and once a day"
01

No screen recording

DEVPROOF never takes screenshots and never reads the content of what you work on.

02

No keylogging

Only when you are active, never what you type. Code, prompts and passwords are not stored.

03

Everything stays local

Your data lives on your PC. Nothing is shared with the client until you export the report yourself.

FAQ

--help Questions and answers

Can’t find the answer? Write to edin@nokto.no.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 14 days. You enter your card in Stripe checkout, but nothing is charged until the trial ends. If you cancel before day 14, you pay nothing.

What is the difference between Solo and Agency?

Solo is for one developer. Agency is for up to five developers on one invoice. The app and features are the same.

What happens after I pay?

You go straight to a page with your licence key and the download. Keep the key safe, for example in your password manager.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now.

Can my client see my data?

No. Everything is stored locally on your PC. The client only sees the report you choose to export and send.

Do I need GitHub?

GitHub provides deliveries and projects automatically. Time from your PC, VS Code and Claude Code is recorded without it too.

Is the app in English?

The app itself is in Norwegian today. Client reports can be created in English or Norwegian.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The subscription is paid one year in advance and renews automatically. Email edin@nokto.no before the renewal date and we stop the renewal. You keep access for the period you have paid for. Periods already paid are not refunded.
